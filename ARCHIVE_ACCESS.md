# Model archive access

The model artifacts are uploaded as an encrypted compressed archive. Use the following questionnaire to obtain the extraction password:

[https://v.wjx.cn/vm/wMNCnEA.aspx#](https://v.wjx.cn/vm/wMNCnEA.aspx#)

The questionnaire collects:

* city;
* organization or institution;
* professional identity or role; and
* industry or sector.

This information is used to understand the geographic, institutional, and professional communities using the research. Do not submit government identification numbers, home addresses, financial information, passwords, API keys, health information, or other sensitive personal data.

Read [PRIVACY\_NOTICE.md](PRIVACY_NOTICE.md) before submitting the questionnaire. For questions about contact the author listed in [README.md](README.md).
