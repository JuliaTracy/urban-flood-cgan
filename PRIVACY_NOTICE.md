# Privacy notice for the model-access questionnaire

## Purpose

The encrypted model archive can be downloaded directly. The online questionnaire is used to provide the extraction password and to understand the communities using the research.

Questionnaire information is used to:

* understand the cities and regions represented among users;
* understand the organizations, professional roles, and industries using the research;
* administer access to the archive extraction password; and
* maintain aggregate, non-identifying research-impact statistics.

## Information collected

The questionnaire collects city, organization or institution, professional identity or role, and industry or sector. Do not submit government identification numbers, home addresses, personal financial information, passwords, API keys, health information, or other sensitive personal data.

## Choice and consequences

Submitting the questionnaire is voluntary. The encrypted archive may be downloaded without completing it, but the extraction password is provided through the questionnaire process. Browsing or cloning this documentation repository does not submit questionnaire information.

## Sharing and retention

Questionnaire records should be accessible only to the corresponding authors and personnel authorized by their institutions to administer the research release. Information should not be sold or published in identifiable form. It may be shared where required by law, institutional policy, research-integrity obligations, or information-security needs.

Records should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to administer access and maintain research-impact records, and should then be securely deleted or de-identified. Before publication, the corresponding authors should confirm any institution-specific data-controller identity, lawful basis, retention schedule, requester rights, and privacy contact required by applicable policy or law.

## Questions

Questions may be sent to the author listed in [README.md](README.md).

## Security note

Submit only the professional information requested by the questionnaire. The extraction password should not be posted publicly or redistributed with the archive.
