# Privacy notice for access requests

## Purpose

Information submitted in a model or source-code access request is used to:

- identify and communicate with the requester;
- understand the roles, sectors, regions, and organizations using the research;
- assess the proposed use and applicable access conditions;
- administer approved downloads and related support;
- maintain aggregate, non-identifying usage statistics and research-impact records.

## Information requested

The request template asks for a name, professional role, sector, country or region, organization, professional contact information, intended use, commercial-use status, and project details. Source-code requests also ask for a justification. Do not submit sensitive personal data.

## Choice and consequences

Submitting a request is voluntary. The authors cannot assess or administer registered access without the fields marked by the template as part of the request. GitHub browsing or cloning of this documentation-only repository does not constitute registration for model access.

## Sharing and retention

Request information should be accessible only to the corresponding authors and personnel authorized by their institutions to administer the research release. It should not be sold or published in identifiable form. It may be shared when required by law, institutional policy, research-integrity obligations, or information-security needs.

Request records should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to administer access, document compliance, and meet institutional or legal record-keeping requirements, after which they should be securely deleted or de-identified. Before launch, the corresponding authors should replace this general statement with any institution-specific controller identity, lawful basis, retention schedule, and rights/contact language required by their institutions and applicable law.

## Questions, correction, and withdrawal

To ask how request information is handled, correct submitted information, or withdraw a pending request, contact the corresponding author to whom the request was sent. Withdrawal does not require deletion where retention is legally or institutionally required, and it does not revoke obligations already accepted for files previously supplied.

## Security note

Email is not designed for highly sensitive information. Submit only the professional information requested in the template. An institutionally managed form or data repository with access controls is preferable if the volume of requests grows.
